01 / 3–4 MINUTES

The symplectic camel

“Can a ball fit through this smaller hole if its volume stays the same?”

  1. Predict. Explain the four coordinates. The cylinder restricts one conjugate plane.
  2. Volume squeeze. Two directions shrink; the other two expand. The determinant is one, yet it fits.
  3. Conjugate squeeze. The ellipse pays for narrowness by growing in the partner direction.
  4. Nonlinear. Press Play. Bending and mixing do not evade non-squeezing.
  5. Two positions. The Lagrangian cylinder changes the question. Now a symplectic squeeze fits.

Land this

Symplectic geometry remembers more than volume. The plane matters.

Do not overclaim: shadow area is a numerical estimate; “outside” counts boundary samples, not four-volume. Containment is analytic for the linear maps. A sampled nonlinear picture is not a proof.

02 / 3–4 MINUTES

Running chaos backwards

“If we reverse every rule, does every pixel have to come home?”

  1. Select a cell. Follow its identity, not just its colour.
  2. Kick. Shift each column by a whole number of cells, wrapping at the boundary.
  3. Drift. Shift each row by its current momentum. Still no collisions.
  4. Undo drift, undo kick. Reverse the order as well as the signs. Every identity returns.
  5. 65,536 cells. Scramble, then Run back. Read the exact-coordinate count, not just the picture.

Land this

A permutation can scramble without discarding state information.

Do not overclaim: the lattice changes the model. Float mismatches are measured in display bins. The exact-byte AArch64 proof excludes the browser, toy, calling loop, float comparison and rendered pixels.

03 / ABOUT 5 MINUTES

Dimples on the Rhine

“Why can one tiny dent on a restless river last so long?”

  1. Observe. Name what is footage and what is a rendered illustration.
  2. Pressure. Spinning water needs an inward push. Lower pressure at the centre gives a dip.
  3. Measuring loop. Freeze the flow. Slide a circulation loop without crossing vorticity; an isolated spinning tube cannot have a loose interior end.
  4. Ring to pair. Opposite surface spins can be the two ends of one tube. This is a possible geometry, not a diagnosis of every river dimple.
  5. Persistence ≠ visibility. Downwelling can strengthen a vortex; a reflection can hide or reveal the same dip.
  6. Evidence. 2023 simulations counted dimples. 2026 laboratory work measured dimple-and-scar coverage. Later spatial statistics show nonlocal correlations.

Land this

A dimple is a clue to the river below, not a complete map of it.

Do not overclaim: nearly level water can converge and spread. The optional planar area-preserving model is not a claim about all river-surface flow.
