Remaining verification boundary

Earlier local checkpoint screenshots covered the six Rhine scenes and a complete lattice echo, but they do not verify this final revision. Camel and final mobile rendering still need that pass.

No new HOL Light proof replay, native AArch64 comparison, benchmark or real-river measurement is claimed. The exact-byte proof boundary is explained in the lattice talk. This bundle does not deploy a website or modify the original voiced films.
