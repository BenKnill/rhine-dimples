Draft 7: the whole film, silent

The full animatic of draft 7, about 7 minutes. There is no audio yet: 56 of the 83 lines are new, so the narration is burned in as subtitles on estimated timing. It opens and closes on our own footage.

What the review changed

  • Footage first and last: normal speed, then a labelled 4× replay onto the real dimples, then the place as a small inset with the date. It ends on the same water at normal speed.
  • Cut: the low-flow detective work (the gauge chart, the calm-evening ranking, the aerial weed-bed guess), the "a line meets the surface" beat and the two-panel recap.
  • New: the moving-reflection experiment. A fixed, gently sloped whirlpool is nearly invisible under a plain sky, until a cloud edge's reflection slides under it.
  • Corrections:
    • circulation is now "speed along each piece × its length";
    • the loose end is marked as hypothetical, and "no spin" is kept separate from "no motion";
    • "a closed ring / a tube can connect to the surface";
    • reconnection is shown as a process, not a clipped ring;
    • no "most arches never make it";
    • obstacle wakes say "under suitable conditions";
    • no numbers on the depth dial;
    • two-way gas exchange;
    • the bridge line is about bent sunlight.
